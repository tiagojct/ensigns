# Ensigns

Ten colour families named after the Pequod and the nine ships she gams with in Moby-Dick. An ensign is the flag a ship flies to show who she is. Each family is one ship's colours.

Status: under construction. This repository is being assembled from five earlier projects: Gam (the site), Pequod, Glauca, Try-Works and Ambergris. Glauca becomes Goney, Try-Works becomes Jungfrau and Ambergris becomes Rosebud.
