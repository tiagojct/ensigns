## 0.1.0 (2026-10-01)

First release of Townho, the family for annotation colours that sit behind or under text: PDF and Zotero highlights, track changes, code and manuscript diffs, review comments and marking student work. It is named after the ship of chapter 54, The Town-Ho’s Story.

- Six semantic highlight fills (claim, result, method, definition, question, limitation) as translucent overlays and opaque swatches.
- Two modes: Golden Inn daylight (warm light paper) and Golden Inn night (warm dark ink).
- Core roles keeping at least 16.9:1 contrast for body text on page grounds.
- Insert and delete inks with decoration rules (underline for insert, line-through for delete).
- Diff roles (added, removed, changed) with greyscale lightness separation exceeding 18 L*.
- Four reviewer colours for collaborative multi-author editing.
- Non-colour tokens in `design` for print hatching patterns and line decorations.
- Environments overlay, cvd and print-grey, passing with zero errors and no waivers across all twenty grounds.
- A specimen with an annotated chapter passage, multi-author track changes and a unified diff.
