All data here is invented. Colour marks a status and nothing else. Every status also has an icon and a word, and every triage level its protocol number, so a status can be read without its colour. The lab results show the flags.

Triage queue

The five Manchester levels, most urgent first. Each row shows the protocol number, the name in English and Portuguese, the place, the wait and the target. White is the class the Grupo Português de Triagem added for entries that need no clinical priority, mostly administrative: it has no target time.

Levels and alarm priorities

The severity levels reuse the triage colours. Orange belongs to triage only.

  • Critical High priority alarm, triage level 1, the only red.
  • Caution Medium priority alarm, triage level 3.
  • Information Low priority alarm, triage level 5.
  • Normal A confirmation. It is the green of triage level 4.
