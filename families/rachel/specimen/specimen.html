Checking your blood sugar at home

Checking your blood sugar shows you how well your treatment is working. Check at the same times each day, and write each number down.

Before you start
  • Wash your hands with warm water, and dry them well.
  • Get your meter and a new test strip ready.
  • Sit down, and rest your hand on a table.
Your target range

Your clinic sets your target range. A reading inside it is fine. Write down every reading, inside the range or not, and bring the list to your next visit.

When to contact your clinician

Phone your clinic on the same day if:

  • your reading is above 13.0 mmol/L twice in a row
  • you feel very thirsty or very sleepy
  • you are being sick

The number is on your appointment letter.
